🧭 Map of the 6 trails

The Agentic OS course has a deliberate structure:T1 founds the mental model, T2-T5 are the 4 layers of the kernel, T6 is the complete deployment. Understanding this map before moving forward saves rework and ensures that each track makes sense in the context of the whole.

🗂️The 6 trails and what each one covers

T1

Fundamentals — Mental Model

Layers thesis, Software 3.0, fault diagnosis, 3 personas, PRATO method. Here you change how you think before touching a tool.

T2

Identity — Kernel Layer 1

CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, scoped rules, governance. Who the agent is, what he can and cannot do.

T3

Knowledge — Layer 2 of the Kernel

Silver Platters, MCP, skills, memory. What the agent knows and how he seeks information.

T4

Workers — Kernel Layer 3

Orchestrator + experts, agentic hierarchy, handoff, multi-agent. Who executes what.

T5

Automation — Kernel Layer 4

Hooks, schedules, observability, evals. The invisible work that gives predictability to the operation.

T6

Implementation — Complete Cases

Marco, Sally and Dr. Sana with end-to-end implementation. From problem to production, step by step.

🏃 Suggested pace

There is no single pace — it depends on your goal.Intense mode for those who want a quick overview; applied mode for those who want to implement while learning.Choose what fits your reality and be consistent.

⚡ Intense Mode (1-2 weeks)

  • •1 trail per day— 2-4 hours of focused study
  • •Focus on understanding the mental model first
  • •Write down questions — implement later
  • •Best for: Founders making stack decisions

🌱 Applied Mode (3-4 weeks)

  • •1 trail per week— study + implement
  • •Each track has a concrete deliverable
  • •Practice before moving on to the next
  • •Ideal for: consultants and builders in production

📊Deliverable by track (applied mode)

  • T1:Fileminha-persona.mdwith your case mapped out
  • T2:CLAUDE.md functional for your context
  • T3:2-3 Silver Platters for the main areas of your business
  • T4:AGENTS.md with orchestrator + 2 experts
  • T5:At least 1 automatic hook working
  • T6:Complete system running, automatic weekly report

🛠️ Prerequisites

The course is about operating, not watching.Without a tool in hand, you will read theory and forget it in 48 hours.Prerequisites are minimal, but mandatory. It takes 15 minutes to set up if you haven't already.

✅What you need to have installed

1. Agentic CLI (choose one)

  • npm install -g @anthropic-ai/claude-code—Claude Code (recommended)
  • npm install -g @openai/codex— Codex CLI (alternative)

2. Account and API Key

  • Anthropic Console:console.anthropic.com— registration + billing
  • Export:export ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=sk-ant-...

3. Open Terminal

  • macOS: Terminal.app or iTerm2
  • Windows: WSL2 + Ubuntu (recommended) or PowerShell
  • Linux: any terminal

4. Git configured

  • git config --global user.name "Seu Nome"
  • git config --global user.email "seu@email.com"

💡No need to know how to program

The course requires you to know how to use a terminal (open, navigate folders, copy commands). It doesn't require you to write code. Skills, hooks and configurations are declarative — you edit text files, not write functions.

🎯 Final project

The final project is not an assessment — it is the real objective of the course.You end Agentic OS with a folder/agentic-osworking: CLAUDE.md, Silver Platters, skills, agents, hooks and at least one automatic weekly report. Without delivery, knowledge evaporates.

📊Minimum viable scope

You don't have to have everything perfect. The final project MVP is:

  • ✓1 CLAUDE.md that clearly defines who the agent is and what he can do
  • ✓2-3 Silver Platters for the main areas of your business
  • ✓1 skill/briefwhich generates weekly report
  • ✓1 working automatic hook (even if simple)
  • ✓1 real weekly report generated and approved by you

💬 duvip.vip Community

Course is theory.Community is where the case becomes collective learning. duvip.vip is the DUVIP community — entrepreneurs, consultants and builders deploying Agentic OS in real projects, with real problems and auditable solutions.

🌐What do you find in the community

  • •Real published cases— members share their Agentic OS: stack, results, learnings.
  • •Peer review of Silver Platters— someone more experienced reviews your .md before you go into production.
  • •Threads by vertical— health, legal, e-commerce, consultancy, tech. Learn from those who have the same context.
  • •Skill library— open skills that members contribute and you can adapt to your case.
  • •Live Sessions— builders showing their system in real time, with questions from the audience.

💡Access

community induclub. Course members have access to the #agentic-os channel where course cases are discussed. Bringing your final project in for review is the best use you can make of the community.

🚀 Next step: Track 2

You have finished Track 1. You have the mental model, you understand the mistakes to avoid, you know the personas, you have mastered the PRATO method and you know how to use the course.Now the real construction begins: Track 2 (Identity)This is where everything takes concrete form — CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, rules and scope.

🎯What will you build on Track 2

  • •CLAUDE.md— the central identity document. Who the agent is, how he acts, what he can and cannot do.
  • •AGENTS.md— the experts’ manifesto. Each agent documented with scope and tools.
  • •Scoped Rules— what each agent can touch, and what is off limits.
  • •Governance— how decisions are made, audited and reversed when necessary.

💬Why identity comes first

It's tempting to jump to tools. Don't do that. An agent without a defined identity will be inconsistent, will cross limits that you don't want him to cross, and will make decisions that you don't approve of. Identity-first is not philosophy — it is engineering.
