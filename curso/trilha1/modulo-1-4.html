📦 Marco — founder SlabHaus

Marco is the co-founder of a live mystery box e-commerce. It operates on three simultaneous fronts: Shopify for orders, TikTok and Twitch for livestream, Meta Ads for acquisition.Every Sunday he spends 3 hours consolidating spreadsheets that should be automated.The pain is classic for the overworked solopreneur.

🏪Marco's Stack and Pain

Current stack

  • • Shopify (orders, inventory, fulfillment)
  • • Meta Ads + TikTok Ads (acquisition)
  • • Twitch + TikTok Live (livestream)
  • • Google Sheets (manual analysis)
  • • Stripe (financial)
  • • Gorgias (customer support)

Main pains

  • • Manual weekly brief (3h every Sunday)
  • • Whale analysis without automation
  • • P&L spread across 4 spreadsheets
  • • No LTV tracking by channel
  • • Inventory decisions on "feeling"
  • • Slow support during livestream

💬How Marco will use Agentic OS

In Track 6, Marco implements:

  • /brief— skill that generates the complete weekly report in 3 minutes, not 3 hours
  • CFO Bot— analyzes P&L, margin per SKU, shipping cost automatically
  • CMO Bot— reads TikTok engagement, identifies whales and product trends
  • Hook Sunday— every Friday at 6pm consolidates data and prepares the following week

⚖️ Sally Tan — boutique law firm

Sally is an associate at a boutique firm specializing in litigation and M&A. Opens 4 to 5 new cases per month, each requiring manual conflict check, folder setup, case law research and team briefing.The environment is regulated, the cost of error is high, and documentation needs to be auditable.

⚖️Sally's Stack and Pain

Current stack

  • • iManage (document management)
  • • Outlook + Teams (communication)
  • • AWS Bedrock (model approved by the firm)
  • • Westlaw (legal research)
  • • Excel (manual billing)

Main pains

  • • Manual conflict check (45 min per case)
  • • Repetitive case folder setup
  • • Slow jurisprudential research
  • • Inconsistent team briefing
  • • No automatic time tracking

🔒Sally's non-negotiable constraint

The office requires that all case data remain in AWS Bedrock (internal VPC), with no calls to unapproved external APIs. This is not a technical limitation — it is a compliance requirement for OAB and customers. The architecture needs to respect this constraint from day one, not as an afterthought.

🔍 Common patterns for all 3

Marco, Sally and Dr. Sana seem to have nothing in common: e-commerce, legal, health. But the structural problems are identical.The solution is the same framework — just with different restrictions in each vertical.This is what makes Agentic OS a system, not a niche product.

🔄The 5 repeating patterns

1. Data spread across multiple systems

Milestone: Shopify + Sheets + Stripe. Sally: iManage + Westlaw + Excel. Sana: EHR + biopsy + billing.

2. Repetitive processes without automation

Milestone: Sunday brief. Sally: case launch. Sana: post-consultation notes. All: weekly hours lost in routine.

3. No cross-system orchestration

None of the three has an orchestrator who coordinates decisions between areas. Connections are manual.

4. Zero audit and traceability

Who changed what, when and why? Without logging, the answer is always "I don't know".

5. Decisions based on intuition, not data

All three make important decisions without the right data dashboard — not because of a lack of data, but because of a lack of aggregation.

🎯 The question becomes a chain

One of the most practical changes to Agentic OS is seeing howa simple question turns into a coordinated chain of experts. This isn't automation — it's intelligent orchestration. Marco asks something, four agents work in parallel, and he receives a summary.

🔗Example: Marco and the whale tier decision

1

Marco asks

"Whale tier is worth the cost of guaranteed pull this quarter?"

2

Orchestrator (Chief-of-Staff)

Identifies: I need financial analysis (CFO Bot) + customer behavior analysis (CMO Bot).

3

CFO Bot responds

Guaranteed pull cost: R$4,200. Average whale LTV: R$18,400. Margin: positive on 3 purchases.

4

CMO Bot responds

Whales with 3+ purchases have 78% retention. Guaranteed pull increases purchase #4 by 34%.

5

Orchestrator synthesizes

Recommendation: implement whale tier. Positive ROI within 60 days based on LTV and retention data.

💡The same pattern for Sally and Sana

Sally asks, "Does this case have favorable precedents for our argument?" → Research Bot searches case law + Case Context Bot reads case documents → summary in 8 minutes. Dr. Sana asks, “Any pending biopsies with a critical deadline?” → Biopsy Monitor + Agenda Bot → prioritized list in seconds.

🪪 Your persona

You are not Marco, Sally or Dr. Sana — butyou have one of three problem structures. Identifying what your case is before moving forward on the trails avoids building the right solution for the wrong problem. This is the only mandatory exercise in the module.

📝Exercise: map your case

Answer these 5 questions before continuing:

1. What is your main stack?

List the 3-5 systems you use most at work.

2. What process do you repeat every week that could be automated?

The equivalent of Marco's brief, Sally's case launch, Sana's notes.

3. What decision do you make in "feeling" due to lack of ready data?

What would you know if your data was prepared as a Silver Platter?

4. What is your non-negotiable restriction?

PHI? Customer data? Proprietary code? Regulatory compliance?

5. What is your quick win?

A 30-minute automation that you could implement this week and see value.

💡Save your answers

These 5 answers are the starting point of your final project. In module 1.6 you will structure this into a complete plan. For now, write it to a fileminha-persona.mdin your project folder.
